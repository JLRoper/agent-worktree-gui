What the round found

  • A ping-pong loop cannot assemble as cut. With every tree cut at the frozen parent, a revise verdict produces a rewrite the assembly must refuse. The driver would park the row pipeline-assembly-conflict on any real ping-pong round.
  • Two ways out: cut a loop visit from its own stage's previous visit (attempt 2), or give the assembly a documented later-visit-wins pick for a path the same stage rewrote.
  • Already right: the cap counts crossings, G1's record is per round and r1 cannot answer r2, and an accept routes nowhere.
  • The gate's refusal sentence is dead document. refuses_when is written on G1 and dropped by normaliseGates, so the driver never applies the note-open condition; only the critic's revise stops a bad draft.
  • The first check is the record's presence, and it is a park, not a refusal: a critique that settles without verdict-r<n>.json dies on pipeline-gate with "the record has not been written" (row 71).
