7. What this page does not claim

  • One walk of one pipeline on one surface in one assistant's queue. Nothing here generalises to the other 11 pipelines in the bundle.
  • Token counts are the panel's per-job totals from jobs.json (input + output). They are not a billing statement, and no cache split is recorded.
  • The proof run's seven seats were scripts. Its 1m 03.3s says nothing about what an agent review costs; this run's 10m 43.6s does.
  • The 27m 53.9s before the tree was cut has no recorded cause. The row has no hold flag and a later row started within 80 seconds, so queue order does not explain it; the record does not say what the panel was doing.
  • Whether the CSS should land is not judged here. The page judges the pipeline's output, not its taste.
  • The pipeline's proof is strong. Calling part of it wasteful is a claim about the parts no later stage reads, not about the parts that make the verdict checkable.
  • Times are UTC as the panel records them; the tree's commits are dated in local time (-0500), so the commit dates and the stage times differ by five hours.
