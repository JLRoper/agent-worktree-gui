Two console gates now assert the one band

agent-worktree-gui, branch wtgui/row-452-is-the-complete-brief-two-3603fa, cut from 20261127_2134_TASK-405_row-405-is-the-complete at 3eaccda0. Measured 2026-11-28 on this tree.
The claim. tools/verify-console-height.mjs and tools/verify-console-resize.mjs now take every room, floor and stop from band() and limit() in public/core/console-grip.js instead of carrying a viewport subtraction of their own. Both read the room in LAYOUT px, at the press's own zoom, and both assert that the console's foot lands on the viewport edge less the gap. A band broken back to the historic mixed-unit formula fails both gates at 90% and 110% zoom, which no 100% reading can see.

What was wrong

The module replaced the old room with one function: band() at public/core/console-grip.js:72 answers min (the sheet's floor) and room (the nearer of the pane's bottom and the viewport's bottom, minus the 8px gap) in layout px, dividing both visual edges by the page zoom once: 

const paneRoom = hasPane ? Number(opts.paneBottom) / z - top - gap : null;   // console-grip.js:82
const viewRoom = hasView ? Number(opts.viewport) / z - top - gap : null;    // console-grip.js:83

limit() at public/core/console-grip.js:96 is the one clamp the move and the release both call. Two gates that exist to guard that gesture still had their own copy of the arithmetic:

  • tools/verify-console-height.mjs measured its fixture's room with Math.round(window.innerHeight - pre.getBoundingClientRect().top - 8). That is the historic formula: innerHeight and the rect are visual lengths, the gesture divides both into layout px at the press, so the gate only agreed with the module at 100% zoom. Its only use of the number was a fixture check (room > 440), so on a zoomed page it would have accepted a console whose real room was smaller: the fixture measured against a number the gesture does not obey.
  • tools/verify-console-resize.mjs asserted none of the band's room: it read the floor off the drag record and expected the pull leg to land on shortStart.h + 260, a number derived from the step count rather than from the module's clamp. A box that stopped on a shorter room would have been reported as a short drag.

A note on the counts in the brief. It quoted verify-console-height failing 30 of 59 and verify-console-resize failing 4. On this tree, before any edit, both passed: 60 and 41 checks. An earlier commit on the turnover branch (5270cac9, "gates: the console height and resize read the one band") had already repaired the pressing and floor halves, which is what those failures were. What it did not remove is the arithmetic above: the height gate still subtracted its own room, and neither gate could see the unit at any zoom but 100%. The canary below is what proves the repair is now complete.

One more change came out of this work and is worth naming: the height gate's grab() now checks the TOP the gesture froze as well as the height it pinned (tools/verify-console-height.mjs:298-302). With the band measured in the fixture, a press that landed after the board's anchor sweep had moved the console froze a top the gate had not measured, and the drag then stopped on a room the gate never read: it reported 380 instead of 420. The press is released and taken again when that happens, and a drag that does end short now prints the band's room and edge beside the height.
