The console corner, read per step

Queue row 281 · agent-worktree-gui · branch wtgui/read-queue-row-281-first-it-carries-the-f9bd09 · 2026-11-23, finished 2026-11-24 00:30 · version 1.166.0 · Chromium via Playwright 1.64.1, Node 26.3.0, headless, 1440x900

The reader's words: grab the agent console's bottom-right handle and drag it DOWN SLOWLY; it misbehaves again. This is the third time. What follows is not a theory about why: it is a per-step reading of the drag in a real browser, the loop the trace names, the change that removes the disagreement, and the gate that now runs that reading on every landing.

1. What was measured, and how

One harness, tools/verify-console-corner-slow-drag.mjs, boots a throwaway panel (tools/panel-testkit.mjs), seeds a docked Chat pane with two turns - one settled, one running - opens both, puts the transcript on its foot (where a reader watching a live turn sits), then presses the console's grip and walks the pointer down 3px every 110ms for 40 steps: 120px of travel over 4.4 seconds. At every step it records the console's height, its rect's top and bottom, its own scrollTop, the transcript's scrollTop, and the grip's own rect. Four shapes are driven:

  • settled - nothing arrives while the hand is down (the drag alone);
  • live - a paragraph streams into the console being resized, one word or two per step, the way the wire sends it;
  • above - a row lands in the turn above the console, so content grows on the other side of the reader's place (40/40 rows fed, asserted, so the shape cannot be vacuous);
  • clipped - the console is shrunk to 300px first, so its content is taller than the box, it scrolls inside itself, and the drag back down is the reader revealing the turn they clipped: the shape where two scrolls (the transcript's and the console's own) both have somewhere to write.

Each reading is taken after the step's pointer move and one frame, so a row says what the page drew for that step - never a before-and-after pair, which is exactly the sampling that missed this defect twice.

3. Which loop moved it: the page's tail pin

The harness wraps Element.prototype.scrollTop and keeps the calling frame of every write to a transcript. The first write of the live run, before this branch:

  • 5116 → 5119
    at HTMLDivElement.set [as scrollTop] (eval at evaluate (:370:30), <anonymous>:107:91) | at setScroll (http://127.0.0.1:60205/board/console.js?v=ec1000f9fc60:3315:16) | at http://127.0.0.1:60205/board/console.js?v=ec1000f9fc60:3414:41
  • 5136 → 5137
    at HTMLDivElement.set [as scrollTop] (eval at evaluate (:370:30), <anonymous>:107:91) | at setScroll (http://127.0.0.1:60205/board/console.js?v=ec1000f9fc60:3315:16) | at http://127.0.0.1:60205/board/console.js?v=ec1000f9fc60:3414:41

That frame is stickCardBodySoon's animation-frame flush in public/board/console.js:3414 (setScroll(b, max) at :3315). It is the panel's "follow the foot" pin for the card's session scroller: the console in the docked pane is drawn at its content height, so the console's own pin has nothing to scroll and the body is the box that follows the stream. When the drag grows the console by 3px, the pin writes the transcript's scrollTop to its new maximum, which moves the console's top edge up by 3px - and the corner is top + height, so the two writes cancel to the pixel.

That is the structural cause the row guessed at, confirmed: two loops own the same box. The drag owns the height; the tail pin owns the transcript's offset; the reader's hand is on the sum of the two. The page's pin is the one that disagrees with the gesture.

The browser's anchor is the other candidate, and it is not the culprit - it is the fix for the shape above

Chromium's own scroll anchoring also adjusts a scrollport's offset, silently, with no scrollTop write for a page to see. It was tried as the second half of the stand-down (a body.is-out-gripping .job-body { overflow-anchor: none } rule) and measured wrong: with the transcript on its foot and a row landing in the turn ABOVE, a still hand lost 86.25px of corner with the anchor disabled, because the browser was the thing holding the reader's place while content grew above it. That rule is not in the change; the sheet says why, and the gate's above shape fails if it is added back.

4. Why this keeps coming back

Three landings, three fixes, one structural mistake repeated. The corner's arithmetic has always been re-derived by hand against "the body under the box this time":

  • PR 780 (0faf5d13, 1.114.1) - tools/verify-console-corner-tail.mjs closed a jitter whose cause was stickSoon correcting inside an animation frame only. The correction moved from a frame to a microtask. The shape did not change: a second loop still wrote the same box.
  • PR 748 - one builder for every readable command line. The body became a different tree with different growth points; the pin calls were re-attached by hand.
  • PR 881 (13a343f) - one stream with thinking rows, the brain pane deleted, the body now the same pre.job-out the tools use. The console's growth and the transcript's growth are now the same event, which is why the page's pin and the drag now write the same number in the same step.

Each rewrite handed the corner a body its arithmetic was not written for, and the gate that covered it (verify-console-corner-tail.mjs, and verify-console-resize.mjs before it) samples the height and the corner at the END of a step. A cancellation is invisible to that sampling: the height is right, the corner is wrong, and neither a before nor an after picture of one step shows the other loop. Nor did either tool ever press DOWN on a transcript that is on its foot: the old tail harness drags UP on a board card, where the pin never fires, and the resize harness drags a card with no session scroller at all.

5. The change, and what it forbids

One owner for the height

The console's height is now --out-h, written only by setOutHeight (public/board/console.js) and read by the sheet's .job-out { height: var(--out-h, auto) }. The three paths that used to write style.height on their own - the grip gesture, the remembered height a rebuild restores (applyStoredHeight), and the float's slack console drag (public/assistant.js) - all call it. There is no second place to read a second number from.

The gesture owns the rect, not just the height

At the press it reads the box's top edge and the pixels of the grip the hand landed inside; every move is pointer + grab - top. The height is no longer "the drawn height plus this move's travel", so a reflow cannot accumulate, and the corner lands on the pointer by construction. The clamps are still applied once, at the release.

The page's tail pin stands down for the drag

cardBodyHeld(body) is asked by stickCardBodySoon (and its queued frame), stickCardBody, stickSoon's flush and stickConsole; while the gesture is live, nothing scrolls the transcript or the console it holds. The browser's anchor is left alone on purpose (section 3). On release, a transcript that was following its foot goes back to it once, through setScroll, so a resize the reader asked for cannot take the stream's follow away - before this branch, a slow drag in the settled shape left the transcript 120px short of its foot and stopped it following.

What this forbids a future console rewrite from doing: writing a height on a .job-out anywhere but through setOutHeight; and re-pinning the transcript (or the console's own tail) while a corner gesture is live. A new body, a new stream shape or a new pane changes what is inside the box - it does not get to invent a second geometry. Both rules are asserted by test/console-grip.test.js (one writer, one variable, no overflow-anchor stand-down, the frozen-top arithmetic) and by the harness below.

6. The gate

tools/verify-console-corner-slow-drag.mjs is declared // HARNESS: LIVE GATE, wired as npm run verify:console-slow-drag, and test/harness.test.js holds every live gate to a package.json script - so it is a rumour the moment that wiring is dropped. npm run verify:all runs it with the rest of the fleet.

What it asserts, per scenario, at every one of the 40 steps:

  • the grip's bottom edge is on the pointer (2px band);
  • no step moves the box backwards under the pointer;
  • the height grew by the pointer's own travel (120px for 120px);
  • nothing re-pinned the transcript's foot while the pointer was down (box top within 1px, and the scrollTop move reported);
  • after the release: the transcript is back on its foot, and --out-h holds the height the gesture chose.

8. Process hygiene

The harness starts one sandbox panel (node server.js) and one headless Chromium, and both are killed on exit - the browser in withBrowser's finally, the panel in panel.stop() after it. Since this row's mandatory item is that no node instance the test starts is left running, the harness now asserts the panel's pid is gone (signal 0, the "does this pid still exist" question on both platforms), so a leak fails the gate instead of being found by a reader later:
