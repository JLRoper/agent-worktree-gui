1. What moved, and what stayed

The brief was one cut: take the lane half out of lib/waves.js into lib/wave-lanes.js, a pure move, with lib/waves.js keeping the re-export of every name its importers read. Nothing schedules, advances or switches anything in either file; the cut is a file boundary, not a behaviour change.

What went to the new file, with the ballast it needs:

  • lib/wave-lanes.js:122 readWaveLane, the per-lane read that answers ok: false rather than guessing when a lane names no tree;
  • lib/wave-lanes.js:224 waveLanes, the lane set derived on every read, plus the file-rule grouping and the tree clash that refuses two lanes on one tree;
  • lib/wave-lanes.js:297 joinWaveLanes, the one-commit join that hands the pinned commits to lib/pipeline-aggregate.js;
  • lib/wave-lanes.js:48 and :49 waveMemberClosed / waveMemberOpen, the closure words the lane fold reads first;
  • lib/wave-lanes.js:27, :34 and :83 - clip, namedAbandon and text, which travel with the lane fold because it asks them of the same records.

What stayed: the wave record, the fold, the gates, the evidence reads and the id derivation. Those are the state half, and the point of the cut is that a lane change and a state change now land in different files.
