The Initiatives pane creeps up one line every poll - measured, named, fixed

The reader's report: the pane slides up one line every few seconds while they leave it alone. It was measured before a line of code changed. The scroller never moves, so this is not a jump to zero: the content above the reader's fold re-flows between polls, and a fixed scrollTop under it shows up as their rows sliding. The fix keeps the reader's card where it was and stops writing an unchanged payload; a new browser gate fails on the old build and passes on this one.

Verdict

The mechanism is (b), and (a) is ruled out. Across 241 samples at 250 ms on an idle pane, scrollTop never changed and the pane's content coordinates never changed either: an unchanged payload lays out identically, so the relative stamps and the clock lines are not re-flowing on their own. What moves the reader is a real change ABOVE their fold - a task row filed or retired in an open fold, a wave landing - which grows the content by one row (53 px measured) between two polls. scrollTop stays where it was, so every row under the change moves down by 53 px, and the reader reads it as the pane creeping up one line. No sample in any run shows a jump to zero.

1How it was measured

tools/measure-initiatives-creep.mjs opens the panel in a real browser, unfolds every card and every assistant and task fold, parks the pane mid-list, and then samples four numbers every 250 ms: the pane's scrollTop, its scrollHeight, the viewport offset of a marker card (vy, its distance below the pane's top edge) and its content coordinate (cy = vy + scrollTop). The two together are what separates the two candidate bugs: if the scroller moves, vy moves and cy stands still; if the content above re-flows, cy stands still and vy moves. It also counts whole-pane rebuilds (a childList record on the pane element itself) and, from the network, the pane's own GET /api/initiatives polls.

Two homes were used. The sandbox is a fixture panel on its own port with its own WAG_HOME, so a store write can be made in the middle of a run without touching the reader's board; the live board on 127.0.0.1:7331 was driven read-only, through --url, and its store was never written.

The cadence the reader sees is the pane's own read: INIT_READ_GAP_MS = 4000 (public/assistant.js:3429), measured as 16 polls in 60 s. The roster poll at public/app.js:3384 (5000 ms) re-renders the same pane from the same painter, which is why the reader's report says five seconds. Both paths land in renderAssistantInitiatives, so both are covered by the fix.

4The fix

Two changes in public/assistant.js, and nothing else in the tree:

  • The scroller is carried across the repaint on the card the reader is looking at (public/assistant.js:4002 to :4072, called at :4153 and :4168). captureInitScroll records scrollTop and the first card that STARTS inside the view with its viewport offset; restoreInitScroll puts that card back where it was by moving the scroll by the difference. Anchoring on a card that straddles the top edge would read a zero correction when the change happened inside that very card - the case measured above - so a card that starts in view is preferred. The anchor is the card's own data-init-card, which is unique in the pane; the rows inside a card carry an assistant id or a time that repeats under every card that has one, and resolving one of those back after a rebuild found a different row and moved the pane 1315 px, measured.
  • An unchanged payload is not written again (public/assistant.js:3430 and :4154 to :4163). Every input the pane draws is in the markup string, so an identical string is an identical DOM. When it differs the pane is rebuilt as before, the fold paint runs, and the task folds are still reconciled in place on every pass.
  • Three reader states are left alone on purpose: at the very top stays at the top (a new initiative appears where they are looking), at the foot stays at the foot, and a card the pass removed falls back to the numeric scrollTop it was captured with.

5The gate: tools/verify-initiatives-creep.mjs

Its own sandbox panel, its own home, and five groups of checks: 60 s alone with the pane parked; a manual scroll that must survive three polls; a fourth wave written into the wave store, which must be drawn without a manual Refresh; a running task filed on the first initiative, which must grow the content above the reader without moving them; and no page or console errors. It counts polls from the network and rebuilds from the pane element, so "the poll still runs" is measured, not assumed.

6What was NOT changed

  • routes/waves.js, lib/waves.js and public/core/waves-lane.js are untouched: git diff --stat on the branch names public/assistant.js and package.json only, plus the new files under tools/ and test/.
  • The poll and the repaint are not silenced. A real change still lands with no manual Refresh - the gate writes a fourth wave into the store and asserts the drawn [data-wave-id], and asserts that the pane rebuilt for it.
