1. The audit: four kinds, and the surface each one keeps

The button counted four kinds, and its Settings tab carried four switches - one per kind - each on by default. The brief describes three switches and lists four names; the markup is the authority, and it had four. The table is what each kind now has, and where the reader meets it.

KindWhat it isSurface after this landingWired at
permissions a run stopped on a command it wants to run; the panel's permission box, or a gateway approval the Permissions control: amber count, popup listing every request, one answer route public/index.html:66, public/app.js:13967
clarify questions a run waiting on a decision: a clarify question on a card the Clarify control: purple count over the question history, rows that open the asking turn public/index.html:46, public/app.js:13914
collisions two live trees touching the same work, nothing stopped yet the Collisions control: violet count of pairs waiting on a person, popup where a pair is ruled public/index.html:55, public/app.js:13944
review items a finished row whose result nobody has checked yet the Review control: amber count from the rows the board draws, a press that filters the table to exactly them public/index.html:68, public/app.js:13704

The choice made for review items: the existing Review control, not a new one. It already counted these rows and already filtered the table to them, so a second control would have been two numbers for one fact. What changed is the copy: its painted title now says it is the header's one count of results to read, and the sentence that promised the H walk is gone because the walk is gone. The count itself is unchanged: wtReviewLane.count(state.rows.values()) (public/app.js:13707) over the rows the board drew, through awaiting (public/core/review-lane.js:25). A delivery whose tree was archived or deleted is not a row, and is not counted - the property the lane module exists to keep.

2. H: removed, and what that costs

H was the rotation's key. With the button gone there is nothing to rotate over, and reassigning the letter would have put a fourth one-key walk beside three popovers that already have their own keyboard model (Up and Down step the rows, Enter opens one, Escape closes). So H now does nothing at all: the listener is deleted, the letter is unclaimed, and the page raises nothing for it. The gate presses h and H on a blurred page and reads the same board, the same closed popovers, no ring and no page error.

What the reader loses, named:

  • the combined badge - one number for everything waiting. Four counts now sit in the header, each naming its own kind;
  • the H walk into the oldest review row: the row opened, the result chip opened onto its card, and ✓ Reviewed under the caret for one more key. Clicking Review filters the table to exactly those rows, and each row keeps its own ✓ Reviewed;
  • the four switches that could narrow the count. Each surviving control counts its own kind unconditionally, which is the same default the switches shipped with.

6. What this page does not prove

  • It does not re-prove the CONTENT of the three popovers: their rows, their answers and their histories are their own gates' subject, and the readings above are those gates' own numbers.
  • It does not measure the reader's loss quantitatively. The H walk is gone; how much that costs a reader who used it is not a number this page can produce.
  • It does not touch the Questions roster the Clarify control owns, or the collision pair card, or the review lane's stored record. None of them moved.
