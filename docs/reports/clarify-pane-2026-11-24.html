The clarify card, where the reader is looking

Queue row #277, agent-worktree-gui, 2026-11-24. A real clarify question driven through the panel's own agent path in a sandbox panel, a real Chromium reading the turn output, and the two worker cards that timed out unanswered today (rows #267, #254) behind it.

The card was not missing. It was drawn where nobody was looking, and then the fold could take it away entirely. A parked question reached the page and was rendered under the console of the turn that asked it - 1 176 px down a 900 px viewport, with nothing scrolling the reader to it, so the only thing that visibly changed was the header's Clarify badge. And the block lived inside turnBody, which is drawn only for an open turn: a reader who had folded the turn, frozen it to a snippet, or turned the pager away drew zero question blocks while the prompt was still parked.

1. What the reader reported

"the question and its choices did not show up in the turn output at all, only the floating Clarify header bar button, so he could not read the question he was being asked to answer. He answered it from the header button only because the choices were also in the queue row."

The clarify history store on this board agrees that questions were being asked and going unanswered. Those rows are the second half of this report: the cards nobody could see.

2. Reproduced with a real card, through the agent path

tools/verify-clarify-pane.mjs boots a sandbox panel on a spare port, points it at a stub Runs API, and starts an agent through the panel's own POST /api/agents. The stub plays a parked turn over real SSE: tool.started clarify, then clarify.request with the question, three choices and a file link, and then leaves the stream open - a parked run's stream stays open, which is what keeps the panel from settling the job. The page is a real Chromium and every number below is read from the DOM it paints.

Before the fix, with the card at the top of its own thread and nothing folded, the block existed and was correct - question text, three clickable choices, the typed box and Send, the link anchored through the panel's own opener. The failure was visibility and survival:

3. Where the card was lost, named in the code

3.1 A question was a detail of the open turn

turnBody ended with questionPrompt(turn, copy) + questionAnswered(turn), and turnBlock called turnBody only when the turn was open - the folded and frozen shapes drew a head, a peek (or a snippet) and nothing else. The pager made it worse: a turn on a page the reader was not reading was not in the DOM at all, so its question could not be either. public/render-jobs.js:209 (turnBody), :268-300 (turnBlock)

3.2 Nothing landed the box

updateJob merges the frame, repaints the card, and restores the reader's scroll position - which is right for output arriving in a console the reader is reading, and wrong for a question that stops the run. The card can be pages down a long board, and a question that arrives while the reader is reading another row is invisible except for the header badge. public/render-jobs.js:846-884 (updateJob), public/app.js:2522 (landClarifyQuestion)

3.3 A parked question did not count as a live turn

turnLive counted a running turn, a starting one, a held one, and any unanswered approval. A question parked on a turn whose status had fallen out of that set - an interrupted turn whose stream was lost while the gateway still held the prompt - made questionPrompt return "" by its own guard, so the card drew nothing even with the reader looking straight at it. public/board-scaffold.js:133-142, public/app.js:2242 (if (!turnLive(turn) || turn.questionDone) return "";)

3.4 A timeout kept no question

closeQuestion wrote { key, closed } and nulled j.question: the card's only record was a one-line "question closed without an answer here" with no question, no choices and no reason. The header's popup still knew what was asked; the place the reader was looking did not. lib/jobs.js:6394 (closeQuestion), public/app.js:2398 (questionAnswered)

4. The fix

  • One block, drawn by the turn. turnBlock takes the question block as an argument and appends it in every shape (open, folded, frozen); turnBody no longer draws it, so there is never a second copy. public/render-jobs.js:268-300
  • Exactly once, even behind the pager. conversationHtml decides the block's one place: the turn's own copy when it is on the page, the pinned copy when the turn is off-page but pinned, and otherwise a block at the top of the card naming the turn it came from. public/render-jobs.js:498-512
  • The question comes to the reader. A frame that carries a new request id calls landClarifyQuestion, once per prompt, and only when the box is not already on screen: it scrolls the box to the middle through programmaticScroll and rings it with the existing jump flash. A landing that re-fired every frame would fight the reader's scroll for as long as the question stayed open, so the request id is remembered in a page-level set. public/app.js:2521-2535, public/render-jobs.js:879-884
  • A parked question is a live turn. turnLive counts an unanswered question, the same rule it already applied to an unanswered approval. public/board-scaffold.js:133-142
  • A closed card keeps what it asked. closeQuestion writes the question, its choices, when it was asked and why it closed onto questionDone; the card draws them as a dead block: no buttons, the reason, the two clock stamps. lib/jobs.js:6394-6420, public/app.js:2398-2424, public/style.css:3410-3424

Answering is unchanged on the wire and still one answer per card: the page's flight map is keyed on the gateway's request id, the panel refuses a second POST with 409 answer_in_flight, and a press clears every copy on both surfaces. The gate presses a choice and reads one POST carrying { answer, request_id, execution_generation }.

7. What this does not cover

  • The approval box is still drawn by the open turn's body, so a folded turn hides it too. It has a second surface the question does not (the dock draws every live permission), which is why it was left alone here - the same fix would make it one block drawn by the turn, and it is the obvious next row.
  • A minimized card still hides its body by design; its head chip says "waiting on your answer" and the press un-minimizes the card and scrolls to the box (existing behaviour, unchanged).
  • An assistant's own question lives in the float, and the float is its only host. The header's Clarify popup row opens that float on the right thread - that path was already right and is untouched.
