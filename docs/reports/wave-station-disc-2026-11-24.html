The connector ran through the wave station's disc

agent-worktree-gui, row 284. The waves lane draws one connector down an initiative card and a station (W1, W2, W3) on it. On the wave the card marks current the line ran into the ring, through the middle of the disc, and out the bottom, while every other station hid it. This page is the defect, the states it can be drawn in, the two-line fix, and the rendered pixels that say so at two pane widths.

1. What the reader saw

The screenshot below is the row's own attachment: the connector is drawn in the --tab-ready blue, the ring is the current station's --tab-run green, and the line is plainly visible inside the ring, entering at the top and leaving at the bottom. The two stations above and below are drawn the same way except for one thing: their discs are opaque.

Reproduced in the harness, on a seeded card whose second wave is the one the card surfaces as the next to open (data-wave-next="W1 done, W2 ready - press to open"), which is exactly the state the reader's W3 was in:

The range is the number that matters: the disc's fill is a flat colour, so any row of the disc that is not one colour has something else in it. Before the fix the current disc's middle two columns rendered as rgb(79, 204, 94) on the ready wave, i.e. the connector seen through a 14% tint, against a disc of rgb(23, 44, 38).

2. Every state, and which of them already hid the line

The states are the wave store's own (lib/waves.js WAVE_STATES), folded on the read. The lane paints the disc from data-wave-state, plus one rule that only the current station gets (data-wave-current). Enumerated against public/style.css: before this change the current rule was the only one whose fill carried an alpha.

So the defect was not a state at all: it was the current mark, which is added to whichever wave the read calls current (the lowest-position running wave, else ready, else unentered - lib/waves.js currentWaveId). The reader's W3 was ready and current, which is why its ring was green, its line blue, and its middle see-through, while a ready wave that was not current hid the line correctly.

3. The fix

Two rules in public/style.css. The disc states its stacking level, and the current disc lays its tint as an image over the opaque surface rather than as the fill itself. Nothing else moves: no colour changes, no size, no state styling.

@@ #as-initiatives .wave-pos
   background: var(--tab-surface);
   letter-spacing: 0;
+  z-index: 2;
 }

-#as-initiatives .wave-row[data-wave-current="true"] .wave-pos { border-width: 2px; border-color: var(--tab-run); color: var(--tab-ink); background: var(--tab-run-bg); }
+#as-initiatives .wave-row[data-wave-current="true"] .wave-pos {
+  border-width: 2px;
+  border-color: var(--tab-run);
+  color: var(--tab-ink);
+  background-color: var(--tab-surface);
+  background-image: linear-gradient(var(--tab-run-bg), var(--tab-run-bg));
+}

The colour is identical by construction: rgba(46, 160, 67, 0.14) over #141922 is rgb(24, 44, 39), which is what the disc renders now; the before run measured rgb(23, 44, 38) for the same fill, one step of rounding apart. The line is still painted below the disc, and now there is nothing translucent left for it to show through.

public/core/waves-lane.js needed no change: the station's markup already carries the wave's state and the current mark, which are exactly what the two rules key on, and the fix is a paint order and opacity change rather than a restyle.

4. The proof: rendered pixels, every state, both pane widths

For every station the harness screenshots a 42x42 clip around the disc, decodes the PNG itself, and reads the pixels. A row of the disc is usable when the four pixels either side of the connector are one colour (that is what a row clear of the label looks like, read from the raster rather than from a font's line box); the label's ink band is then every row between the first and last inked row. On a usable row, the five pixels through the connector must be one colour too. Left image is before, right is after; the samples are 42 x 42 at 1 device pixel, shown at 2x.

What the two tables say together:

  • The line never appears inside a disc. After the fix every station, in every state and at both widths, samples 0 stray pixels in the connector's three columns and a largest channel range of 1 or 0 across the disc. Before, the two current stations sampled 6 and 6 and 6 and 6 pixels and a range of 160 and 190 and 160 and 190.
  • The ring is still legible. All four cardinal points carry the stroke in every state, the stroke's colour is the one its token composites to over the disc (measured to within 6 per channel), and the contrast from the disc is unchanged to 2 decimal places: 1.73:1, 7.63:1, 1.91:1, 1.68:1, 2.14:1 across the states.
  • The line still meets both edges of the ring. Just above and just below the ring, in the connector's two columns, the pixel is the state's own connector colour (--tab-run for running, --tab-ready for ready, --tab-accent for done, and so on) in every station at both widths, so the route reads as one continuous line with a station sitting on it rather than a line with a gap.
  • It is not a restyle. The runs were compared dot for dot: the largest interior shift is 1/255 and the largest ring shift is 0/255, and every ring's contrast is within 0.05 of what it was.

5. How the gate is run

node tools/verify-wave-dot-opacity.mjs [--shots <dir>] [--json <file>] [--baseline <file>]
npm run verify:wave-dot-opacity
node --test test/wave-dot-opacity.test.js

The harness boots its own panel in a throwaway directory, seeds one wave per state plus the surfaced wave, opens the Initiatives tab at a 1440px and a 2200px window (a 479px and a 1099px pane), and samples every station. It leaves the live panel on 7331 and ~/.wag untouched, and it prints BROWSER-HALF-NOT-RUN and exits 2 when Playwright is not installed rather than passing a half that never ran. The unit test points the same sampler at rasters whose answer is known, including a disc with the line painted through it, so a blind sampler cannot make the live gate green.

The two runs quoted here are in docs/reports/wave-station-disc-2026-11-24/before.json and after.json: every station, its box, its sampled rows, its colours and its verdict.

6. Limits, stated

  • The label's own rows are not sampled: the glyph covers the middle of a 22px disc, and on the 2px current ring only the three or four rows above and below its ink are clear. That is enough to catch a line that spans the whole disc, which is what the connector does, and the unit test proves the sampler sees a stripe on those rows.
  • The empty, unentered and ready rings are drawn from --tab-line-strong, an opaque hairline, and read 1.4 to 1.7:1 against the disc. That is the design's own contrast for a not-yet-active station, it is unchanged by this fix, and it is reported here rather than restyled: a restyle was explicitly out of scope.
  • The measurements come from one rendering (Chromium, device scale 1, on this machine). The claim they support is the paint order and the opacity, not a guarantee about every engine's antialiasing.
