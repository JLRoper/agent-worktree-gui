The console's one size, on an assistant command card

An assistant command card is the panel's own text drawn inside the console: a SPAWN head, its TODO bullet rows, a bold Prompt: label under them and a category glyph. The head inherited the console's size, but the bullet rows, the label and the glyph were pinned at 12px, so a console the reader Ctrl+wheel-zoomed carried a card that stayed behind, at 12px against 24px of console. The card now resolves from the same --term-size as the console around it: Settings and the wheel write ONE property, so a zoom cannot drift out of step with the reader's own size. Every number below is getComputedStyle after a real Ctrl+wheel event dispatched over the console that holds the card.
