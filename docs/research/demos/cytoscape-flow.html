    Tap a stage — or press one of the buttons above — to open it here.

    Why this page is the comparison and not the recommendation. cytoscape is faster, smaller than mermaid and actively maintained, and its dagre layout routes the loop cleanly. But the drawing is one <canvas>: nothing in it has a name, a role, a tab stop or a DOM node, so the keyboard path and the accessible list under the picture are a second, separate implementation that has to be kept in step with the picture by hand — and the panel's own browser gates read DOM nodes.

    The status line prints the painted label size as font-size × zoom: cytoscape fits the graph to its container by default, so a 12 px label can be painted at 8.6 px without anything looking wrong.
