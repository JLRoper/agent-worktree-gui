Press a stage — or Tab to one and press Enter — to open it here. The drawing marks it current and the panel under it says what the stage asks a worker to do and which exit check ends it.

What this page is. mermaid, not a mermaid-shaped drawing: the diagram is seven lines of text built from pipeline-definition.js, and every interaction on top of it — the click, the Tab stop, the mark, the accessible name — is post-processing the SVG mermaid handed back. That is the cost of the recommendation, and this page exists to show it rather than hide it.

The status line reads the painted DOM: the drawing's width, the smallest label actually rendered, how much of it is past the card's edge. Change the window width and nothing recalculates until you press a stage-count button — mermaid re-renders the whole diagram, it does not re-lay it out.
